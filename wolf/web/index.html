            Help

            How WOLF works
            • It learns as you talk. When you mention something worth keeping (a goal, a preference, someone in your life), WOLF saves it. You'll see a small "Saved to memory" tag.
            • You're in control. Everything it knows is on the Memory tab, and you can delete any of it.
            • It stays with you. Your memories are files on your own computer, not in the project.
            • It won't keep secrets. WOLF never saves passwords, card numbers or similar.
            • Starting fresh saves your progress. "Start a new conversation" (or 20 quiet minutes) wraps up the chat and writes a line in your journal below.
